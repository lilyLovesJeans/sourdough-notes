Sweet Stiff Starter

How to reduce sourness in sweet dough? This is one of the most common question I get all the time. In making bread such as Shokupan, burger buns, dinner rolls etc, we often do not prefer it with a hint of tang. Sweet stiff starter(sss) is what I have been using in sweet dough to cut the sourness.

Lets quickly revisit sss :-

(1) what is sss?
It simply means starter that is fed with lower hydration and with sugar.

(2) When do we use sss?
When we want to reduce or eliminate the sour notes in our bread. This usually happens in sweet dough (not limited to enriched dough)

(3) How does sss work?
By inducing sugar into the starter, it creates osmotic stress and limits the bacteria cell generation. This in turn limits acid built-up and reduces/eliminates sourness.

(4) Why does my bread still taste sour even with sss?
You need to use active liquid starter to build sss for it in order to withstand the osmotic stress. Hence it’s best to refresh your liquid starter a few times prior of using it.

(5) What is the composition of sss
My preference is
hydration – 50-55%
sugar – 15-25%
** % based on the flour fed

(6) What is the pros of SSS besides reducing/eliminating the sour notes in bread?
• sss gives a chewier and more shreddy texture
• sss stays at peak longer than liquid starter, so there is a larger window/flexibility on when it can be used

(7) What is the cons of SSS?
• More cumbersome to prepare

(8) Do we need to maintain a sss all the time?
No, build sss as and when needed in recipe

幫我解讀 酸種不酸的關鍵

下面把你提供的內容 用最清楚、最白話 的方式解讀成 👉 「酸種麵包如何做到不酸」的真正關鍵

🍞 酸種不酸的關鍵（核心重點解析）
要讓酸種麵包 不酸、尤其是 奶油餐包、漢堡包、shokupan、甜麵包 這類甜質高的麵團，關鍵就在：
⭐ 使用 Sweet Stiff Starter（甜硬種 / SSS）

🔍 SSS 的作用原理（最關鍵的科學）
甜硬種能讓麵包不酸，是因為：
① 加糖 → 產生滲透壓（osmotic stress）
這個壓力會讓乳酸菌「不舒服」、繁殖變慢， 而造成酸味的乳酸菌、醋酸菌 都被壓制住。
👉 結果：酸生成變少 → 麵包不酸

② 低含水（50–55% hydration） → 酵母比較強、細菌比較弱
低水量會讓酵母比較容易活躍， 但會讓細菌不容易產酸。
👉 結果：酵母照樣膨脹，但酸味大幅下降。

🎯 所以酸味減少的關鍵就是這兩點：
🔑 1. 低水量（stiff）
🔑 2. 加糖（sweet）
讓細菌受壓抑、減少酸，酵母仍可正常發酵。

❗ 那為什麼有些人做 SSS 仍然會酸？
這是大家最常犯的錯。
✔ 原因：你的液種（liquid starter）本身太弱了！
如果液種本身 太酸、太久沒餵、活性差， 你再加糖 → 它直接被壓垮。
→ 酵母死掉 / 變弱 → 細菌反而存活（它們比較耐壓） → 結果：還是酸。
👉 解法：使用前先把液種 refresh 2–3 次（讓它變活）

📌 SSS 的正確組成（最常用比例）
成分
%（以餵入的粉量為基準）
水
50–55%
糖
15–25%
麵粉
100%

⭐ SSS 的其他優點
除了不酸之外：
• 麵包更 有嚼勁、絲絲可撕（shreddy texture） 
• 峰值保持時間長 → 彈性更大，不怕錯過時機 

❗ SSS 的缺點
• 需要多做一步、比較麻煩 
• 不能像液種每天維護 → 隨用隨建 

🎉 一句話總結：酸種不酸的秘密
用旺盛的液種去建立「低水＋加糖」的甜硬種（SSS）。 糖會壓制細菌 → 酸味減少； 低水量讓酵母更強 → 麵團發酵仍順暢。
